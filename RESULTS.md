## Key Takeaways

- In-sample model families (linear, regularized, tree-based, boosting) do not consistently outperform naive baselines on held-out test data, suggesting a strong random-walk characteristic in daily price levels.
- CEAT is the exception where a simple SMA5 baseline achieves marginally better RMSE than Naive (2.160 vs 2.162), though R² remains approximately 0 and negative for all models.
- JK Tyre, Apollo Tyre, and MRF show Naive as the most competitive by RMSE; more sophisticated models may require alternative targets (e.g., returns), additional features, or different horizons to surpass baselines.
